Option 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

You can permanently delete your NutriGlobe account directly within the Android application with immediate effect. No waiting periods, tickets, or human intervention are required:

1 Open NutriGlobe 2.0 on your Android handset and ensure you are signed in.

2 Tap on the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar, or select Settings from the top right menu.

3 Select Account & Security Settings.

4 Scroll down to the red danger zone and tap Delete Account & Data.

5 Read the confirmation warning explaining the permanent nature of the wipe, enter your account credentials to authenticate ownership, and confirm.

What Happens When Your Account is Deleted?

Once confirmed, our client-side cascading deletion engine performs an atomic purge across all systems:

Data Category Action Taken Residual Retention
Cloud Firestore Subcollections
(diary, water, weights, measurements, custom_foods, favorites, planner, shopping)		 All documents across all 8 subcollections are permanently deleted. 0 seconds (Permanently destroyed)
User Profile Document
(/users/{userId})		 Target calories, macros, units, and profile metadata deleted. 0 seconds (Permanently destroyed)
Local SQLite Database
(nutriglobe2_user_{uid}.db)		 Local Room database file is securely wiped and unlinked. 0 seconds (Wiped on device)
Firebase Authentication User record, login session, and OAuth tokens are permanently revoked. 0 seconds (Purged from Firebase Auth)
Cached Preferences DataStore session caches and temporary export files are cleared. 0 seconds (Cleared)

Important Note on Subscriptions

Deleting your NutriGlobe account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. In compliance with Google Play Store rules, subscriptions must be managed directly in the Google Play Store app > Subscriptions.

Option 2: Web / Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the NutriGlobe application, lost access to your Android device, or are unable to access the in-app deletion menu, you can submit a manual account deletion request directly to our developer team:

Send an email from your registered NutriGlobe account email address to:

wasifrizvi111@gmail.com

Subject line: NutriGlobe Account Deletion Request
Body: Please include your full name and confirm that you request the permanent erasure of your account and all associated cloud and local data.

We verify the sender's identity to prevent unauthorized deletion requests. Once verified, all associated records will be permanently purged within 48 to 72 business hours, and a confirmation email will be sent back to you.
